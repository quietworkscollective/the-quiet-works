# CLAUDE.md — Creative Builder Contract

Project: **UNHELD** (public name, `unheld.space`). Its immersive 3D world is **THE QUIET WORKS**; initial chapter **HE'S FINE.**. Human founder and editor has final say on emotional truth and public release. Brand surfaces (titles, metadata, wordmark) say UNHELD; in-world surfaces stay The Quiet Works. Visual identity: premium, cinematic, understated, timeless — never a conventional mental-health website.

## Character

Surreal, beautiful, tactile, occasionally darkly funny, emotionally observant. No sentimentality, generic mental health wallpaper, bleak horror as a default, or motivational corporate writing. A machine should first be a delightful interactive toy and only then an emotional metaphor.

## Design grammar

- Invent a concrete *verb* for each room: stamp, polish, crank, balance, tune, fold, stack.
- Each verb must have satisfying anticipation, actuation, material response, sound, reset and an unexpected alternate interpretation.
- Three discovery paths wherever possible: engage, investigate, stop. Neither one is the 'correct man' answer.
- Short memo writing: one image, one human tension, one turn of phrase; 30–70 words typical. Not every memo should be poignant.
- Use physically grounded easing, precise sound placement and intentional negative space.
- Colour is narrative: bureaucracy/ambition starts amber/teal; connection can introduce organic warmth. Don't map darkness mechanically to mental illness.
- Play and accessibility are equally art-directed.

## Production discipline

1. Read the task packet and specific blueprint chapters.
2. Propose the scene intent and state-machine states in 5–10 lines, then build.
3. Produce a playable browser build early; check an actual screenshot/contact sheet rather than imagining it looks good.
4. Test on touch/mobile and keyboard path. Check memory cleanup and reduced-motion behaviour.
5. Build runtime sound with Web Audio, using Claude Motion only for separate pre-rendered assets, trailers and concept clips.
6. Keep assets licensed and source-preserved. Use licensed ready-made assets (CC0 first, CC BY with recorded attribution) or original Blender models built from committed scripts; procedural geometry where it serves the room. Light is baked in Blender (`docs/ADR/0008-baked-light.md`).
7. Invite Codex independent review on nontrivial diff. Treat its concerns seriously; escalate story disagreements to human editor.
8. Report exact commands run, evidence, changes and unresolved problems. Do not call something finished without validation.

## Creative benchmark for Chapter Zero

Certification Office: pressing the stamp 4 times should be intrinsically satisfying and funny, the 5th should create a surprise, and finding the exit should not require grinding. Deliver a complete six-sense feeling (visual, input, momentum, sound, spatial scale, silence) without trying to force a particular emotion.

## Do not do

- No infinite generative self-help conversation.
- No AI-generated pretend suicide letters or provocative crisis storytelling.
- No 'you're fixed' achievement or mental score.
- No copyrighted voice cloning or unlicensed recording.
- No production deploy, auto-merge, unrestricted remote agent execution.
- No public origin reveal beyond intentionally authored creator materials.
