# Code of conduct

UNHELD is an artwork about what people carry without saying so. The people who make it, and the
people who write to it, deserve the same care the work asks for.

## Expected

- Be kind and specific. Critique the work, never the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming.
- Respect privacy: never share someone else's story, identity or circumstances, and do not post
  your own personal crises here. This repository is public and is not a support service.
- Keep discussion on the work.

## Not accepted

Harassment, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery, personal attacks, trolling,
publishing others' private information, mocking anyone's distress, and sustained disruption.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to info@unheld.space.
Reports are read by the maintainers only. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn,
temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, in proportion to the harm. Decisions can be
appealed to the same address.

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
